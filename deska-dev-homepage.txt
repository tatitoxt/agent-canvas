### One canvas for the whole project

Open a folder and every tool opens beside it: editor, terminal, browser, notes and your agents.
Arrange them once and they stay put.

### Your agents, right next to your work

Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Cursor, Antigravity, Pi, Hermes, Mistral Vibe, Kimi Code, and Deska
Harness work inside your workspace, beside the files they change. When one needs something, it asks
you right there.

Your agents, one desk

### Dex coordinates your agents

Tell Dex what you need, out loud or in writing. He splits the work, hands each part to the right
agent, keeps track of it and tells you when something is ready or needs you.

[See how Dex works][17]

### What your agents make, kept

Pages, documents, designs and notes are saved in one gallery, with every version. Share one with a
link and the comments come back to the same place.

[See artifacts][18]

### What you repeat, Dex does for you

Dex runs a piece of work at the time you say, every week, or when something changes. Every run is
kept in its history, and it asks you when it needs you.

[See automations][19]

Artifacts and automations

## What your agents make stays.An automation keeps it up to date.

A report, a page or a design an agent finishes is kept as an artifact: one place with every version,
a link to share and the comments of the people who read it. Ask Dex to repeat it, and an automation
does the same work on schedule and adds the new version to that same artifact.
* Find it in the gallery instead of scrolling an old chat, with every version since the first.
* Share a link that opens with your computer off. People with the link comment, and the agent makes
  the change.
* Automations run at a set time, on a repeating schedule or when something changes, keep every run
  and tell you when they finish.

Automations run on your computer while Deska is open, even with every window closed. Publishing is
always a separate step: a new version is never public by itself.

## Your team, right there with you.People and agents on the same work.

With Deska Teams your people work in the same Deska: one board where each ticket belongs to a person
or to an agent, comments on what the agents made, and a team computer that keeps working after
everyone closes their laptop.
* Give a ticket to a person or to an agent. Giving it to an agent starts the work.
* Comment on what an agent made and send the comment to it. It answers with the next version.
* A team computer, included and always on, where the whole team’s agents work.

Seats from $29 per person a month, minimum 2 people. Invite by email or with a link, as Owner, Admin
or Member.
